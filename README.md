📖 Tutorial — How to Create Your Own Discord Bot

1️⃣ Create a Discord Application

Go to the Discord Developer Portal and log in with your Discord account.

Create a New Application and give your bot a name.

2️⃣ Create the Bot

Open your application and go to:

Bot → Add Bot

Your Discord bot has now been created! 🤖

3️⃣ Customize Your Bot

You can change things such as:

- Bot username
- Bot profile picture
- Bot description
- Bot settings

Make your bot look the way you want! ✨

4️⃣ Get Your Bot Token

Go to the Bot section and generate/reset your bot token.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Never share your bot token with anyone or upload it to GitHub. Treat it like a password.

5️⃣ Invite Your Bot

Go to:

OAuth2 → URL Generator

Select:

- "bot"
- The permissions your bot actually needs

Copy the generated invite URL and open it in your browser.

Choose your Discord server and authorize the bot.

6️⃣ Start Your Bot

Follow the setup instructions for your bot project, configure your bot token using an environment variable, and run the bot.

🎉 Your own Discord bot is ready!
